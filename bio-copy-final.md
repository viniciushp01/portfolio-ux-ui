## 3. LinkedIn — About / Sobre

**EN:**

I help SaaS products (B2B and B2C) grow through better acquisition and retention — translating business logic into interfaces that are simple, clear, and genuinely useful for the end user.

My background in psychology shapes how I work: before proposing a solution, I build well-formed research hypotheses and run interviews that surface the real problem behind a request — not just the one stated in the brief. That's not "having studied psychology" as a footnote; it's a concrete skill I apply to every research phase.

What I bring to a team:
- Clarity — translating business logic into user experience
- Listening — genuine research, well-formed hypotheses before deciding
- Persistence — I don't stop until I've solved the right problem

I've spent the most time in wellness and fitness products, working with gamification logic for acquisition and retention. I'm most useful on small/mid-size SaaS teams where designers stay close to product decisions — where I can see how a single onboarding change moves a retention number.

I work best focused on one project at a time, taking the space to think before deciding. If you're building a SaaS product and acquisition or retention is the bottleneck, let's talk.
